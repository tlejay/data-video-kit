# Hooks, frame 1 and story shapes

Detail for SKILL.md step 4. Technique menu: `storytelling.md`. Series rotation record: `series-log.md`.

## The hook and the story shape (the first 3 seconds decide everything)

Two lessons: the store-network openers were weak (a conditional "ถ้า..." / "if...", 6 to 7 s
before anything landed), and an experienced editor's rule: **BLUF, Bottom Line Up Front.
The first 3 seconds carry the verdict of the whole video**; whoever cares stays for the why.
Full technique menu: `storytelling.md`.

Rules:
- **Verdict inside 3 s, under ~25 Thai characters spoken.** Test: mute everything after
  second 3. Does the viewer know the conclusion? A teaser fact is not enough.
  The economy video v1 failed: it opened on "ภาษี 1 ใน 4 มาจากโคราช" (a quarter of the tax
  comes from Korat) while its real point ("the economy grows but new homes fell 73%") arrived
  at 45 s. BLUF version: "โคราชเก็บภาษีได้หนึ่งในสี่ของอีสาน บ้านใหม่กลับหายไป 73%".
- No "ถ้า...", no "วันนี้เราจะพาไปดู" (today we'll take you to see), no greeting, no setup clause.
- **Give the answer, hold back the why** (open loop), and pay it off before the end.
- **Frame 1 is a thesis card for the whole video**: someone scrolling past must know what
  the clip is about and how the two halves connect, from the first frame. One or two big
  lines that tie the subject to the conclusion (cassava video: "โคราชปลูกมันมากที่สุดในไทย /
  แต่ทางออกไปท่าเรือ ยังมีแค่รถบรรทุก", Korat grows the most cassava in Thailand / but the
  only way to the port is still by truck, with a root and a truck joined by a road).
  Readable in 2 to 3 s, no draw-in, voice optional; it then leaves and the hook scene plays.
  Picture gives the map, voice pulls the viewer in, both at once.
  Make it a full-bleed **poster**, never a text box on a plain background (a text card does
  not stun, it has to be read). **Before any video build, show the editor three frame-1
  stills (A/B/C) and build only the one they pick** (choosing from stills first saves a
  whole render round).
  > Example (agriculture video): the editor picked B, field over asphalt with a truck queue.
- **A question in the voice is answered inside the first 5 s.** Ask, then answer in the same
  breath ("ยาเม็ดที่คุณกิน ยึดเป็นเม็ดได้ยังไง? ด้วยแป้งมันสำปะหลัง ...", how does your pill
  hold together? cassava starch). A hook that only states a teaser, or a question left
  hanging past 5 s, loses the scroller.
- **True, not clickbait.** Never promise what the video does not deliver.

**Two rules are fixed, the shape is not** (not every topic fits one template). Fixed:
something worth stopping for lands inside 3 s, and the opening promise is paid off. BLUF
fits videos with a clear verdict (most data stories). Pick another shape from
`storytelling.md` when the reveal *is* the content: a guess or ranking ("which district has
the fewest?" answered at the end), a story told in order, a how-to (show the finished result
first, then the steps), or an open question with no verdict yet (lead with the sharpest
contradiction instead). Name the chosen shape in the script draft.

**Default story shape for 60 s** (data video with a verdict; scale the seconds):

| Seconds | Job | Technique |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 | the verdict | BLUF + hook |
| 3 to 15 | why it matters, raise the question | curiosity gap |
| 15 to 45 | the evidence, strongest first, in three beats | inverted pyramid + rule of three, a micro-cliffhanger between beats (rotate the device, see below) |
| 45 to 55 | the answer to the question from 3 to 15 | payoff |
| 55 to 60 | what the viewer should think or do | question / CTA + source |

**Rotate the pattern across the series.** The editor noticed that every clip turned on
"แต่" (but): posted back to back, the same turn in every clip becomes a boring pattern. One
"แต่" turn is fine inside one clip; the same turn in every clip of a series is not. Clips in
a series are also experiments to learn **which shapes the audience likes**, so:
- Before choosing the hook and shape, read `series-log.md` and pick a **hook pattern, story
  shape and pivot device that differ from the last two clips**.
- Pivot devices to rotate instead of "แต่...": a guess then the reveal ("ลองทายดู ... จริงๆ
  คือ"), a ranking countdown, a before/after in time ("สิบปีก่อน ... วันนี้"), a zoom from one
  person/object out to the province, a comparison with another province, a how/why chain
  ("เพราะ ... เลย ... ก็เลย"), a myth then the fact, an open question with no turn at all.
- Examples in this file avoid "แต่" on purpose: examples steer the writing more than rules do
  (the old table had "แต่" in 4 of 5 hooks and in the default shape, the likely source of the habit).
- Use "แต่" at most once per script, and never as the main turn two clips in a row.
- After delivery, append one line to `series-log.md` (date, file, hook pattern, shape, pivot
  device); add the editor's or the audience's reaction when it comes in, so the log becomes
  the "what works" evidence.
